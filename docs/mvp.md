# CatMap MVP

## Positioning

Spot neighborhood cats, collect rare sightings, and help reunite lost cats with their owners.

## V0 Scope

- Capture a street-cat sighting with photo, rough location, and timestamp
- Show nearby sightings on a simple map or feed
- Let owners post missing cats with a photo and last-seen area
- Let users flag a sighting as a possible match
- Notify owners about potential matches

## Product Rules

- No exact public home addresses
- No indoor-cat posting
- No encouragement to approach or handle cats
- Keep automated matching rule-based until the core loop is working

## Build Order

1. Sighting capture
2. Nearby sightings map and feed
3. Missing-cat creation
4. Manual match flow
5. Alerts and moderation
